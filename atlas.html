Your AI Chief of Staff.

An Operating Layer for Your Life

Atlas is a private intelligence built around one person — you. It knows your world, remembers what matters, and gets things done across your work and your life, by voice or by text. Not a chatbot, but the operating layer that quietly keeps everything moving.

Built by ROI Technologies, Atlas applies the same results-driven engineering behind our consulting work to the hardest problem of all: giving one person back their time.

What Atlas Does

One intelligence across everything you have to keep track of.

One Connected Intelligence

Atlas links your time, conversations, people, world, and knowledge into a single layer — so nothing lives in a silo and nothing falls through the cracks.

Natural Requests

Ask the way you'd ask a person — "Book the same restaurant we went to after Alex's graduation." Atlas supplies the context most assistants need you to spell out.

Hands-Free Voice

Talk to Atlas while you drive. It can even place a call on your behalf — like phoning ahead to a restaurant to say you're running late.

Proactive by Design

Atlas raises what matters before you ask — a "leave by 8:11" traffic alert, a booking it can make for you, or a reminder that a promised document is still in drafts.

Memory That Compounds

Not just chat history — a living model of your people, places, preferences, and commitments that gets more useful every single day.

Private & Under Your Control

Each Atlas is its own isolated application and database. You control what it can see and do, important actions need your approval, and every action can be traced.

Yours Alone

Private because of how it's built — not because of a setting.

Atlas isn't a shared service with your data in a column somewhere. It's a single intelligence built around one person, with its own application and its own database. It learns your world, works across your accounts, and follows through on your behalf — while you stay firmly in control of what it sees and what it's allowed to do.
